Create a delphi unit to parse .dlg files from Arcanum.

The  format is as follows: (Example dialogue snippet)
// NODE: Node001
// DESCRIPTION: Initial node, right at the start.
{1}{So... you ve come. The winds of fate have blown you here, as I knew they would. Woodham stands on the brink of ruin, and I cannot face what s coming alone. Will you stand with me against the darkness?}{So... you ve come. The winds of fate have blown you here, as I knew they would. Woodham stands on the brink of ruin, and I cannot face what s coming alone. Will you stand with me against the darkness?}{}{1}{}{}
{2}{I don t trust easily. Why should I help you?}{}{5}{}{11}{}
{3}{If there s a danger to Woodham, I ll fight by your side.}{}{5}{}{21}{}
{4}{This sounds like a mess I should avoid. Why not handle it yourself?}{}{5}{}{31}{}
{5}{You seem... different. What exactly are we up against?}{}{5}{}{41}{}

The first line is the npc text. First field {1} is the line number, second field is the npc text for the male player, third field is the npc text for the female player, fourth field is usually empty, the fifth is optional voiceover number, if no voiceover is present, this field is empty. the sixth field is also usually empty and the seventh field is node actions (likw "gf1 1" "gv1 1" etc.)

The lines after the first one are player options, the format for these is as follows:

First field {2} is the line number for the player option
Second field is the player option text
Third field is the gender check, 0 for male, 1 for female, empty for both genders
Fourth field is intelligence check (5 or -4 or any other number)
Fifth field is player option conditions as a comma separated string like "gf1 1, gv1 1" etc. can be parsed in as a string
Sixth field is the target line number to jump to when the player option is selected. 0 means exit dialogue, non 0 means jump to line x, below zero means call script at line x
Seventh field is player option actions as a comma separated string like "gf1 0, gv1 0" etc. can be parsed in as a string

also note that each line and their corresponding fields should be parsed from between the curly brackets

Format for the dialogue structure should be like this:

Node
	LineNumberStart
	NPCTextMale
	NPCTextFemale
	VoiceOverNumber
	NodeActions
	PlayerOptions
		PlayerOptionText
		GenderCheck
		IntelligenceCheck
		Conditions
		TargetLineNumber
		Actions
	PlayerOptionCount
